A simple place to start

V60 Pour Over

Ratio 1:16 · Grind medium-fine · Water 94°C

Bloom with twice the coffee’s weight in water, then pour in slow spirals. Total brew time around 2:45.

French Press

Ratio 1:15 · Grind coarse · Water 96°C

Steep four minutes, break the crust gently, skim, then press slowly. Let it settle thirty seconds before pouring.

Espresso

Ratio 1:2 · Grind fine · Yield 36g / 28s

Dose 18g in the basket. Aim for a steady, tiger-striped stream and a honey-thick crema.
